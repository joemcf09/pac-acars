Flying on Unicom — no controller online

What to do and what to say on VATSIM or IVAO when there's no ATC: dead silence, everyone self-announces.

UNICOM 122.800
VATSIM airport-focusedIVAO also reports enrouteBoth applies to either network

1 First: is anyone really off?

  • Check who's online in your pilot client or on the map. A higher sector (Approach, Centre/Control, even FSS) may still cover your airport — if so, call them, not Unicom.
  • Only use Unicom when no controller covers you.
  • Re-check every so often. A controller can log on mid-flight; when they do, contact them.

2 Golden rules

  • 122.800 is shared by everyone in range. Keep every call short.
  • You're informing, not requesting. No one answers, and that's normal — expect silence.
  • Announce before you act: taxi before you move, line-up before you enter the runway.
  • English only. Times in UTC (Zulu).
  • Transponder on Mode C (ALT/TX) so others' TCAS sees you.
  • In text your callsign is added automatically. In voice, say it yourself.

The calls, phase by phase

Pattern: “[Field] traffic, [who you are], [what you're doing], [field].” Fill in the blanks. Text examples drop the callsign (the client adds it).

Before taxi / pushback Both

Say where you are, the runway you expect and your taxi route.

KCVG traffic, PAC1235, pushing back stand C4, then taxi for runway 36C, KCVG.
// text: KCVG twr offline — pushback C4, taxi 36C
Entering the runway / departure Both

Call before you enter the runway, then as you roll.

KCVG traffic, PAC1235, lining up and departing runway 36C, northbound, KCVG.
// text: KCVG — lining up 36C, departing N'bound
Clear of the runway / after landing Both
KCVG traffic, PAC1235, clear of runway 36C, taxiing to the cargo ramp, KCVG.
Enroute — position & level reports IVAO

IVAO treats Unicom like TIBA: report your position and intentions enroute, not just at airports. Announce especially with traffic within 20 NM (check TCAS), and:

  • ~10 min before a reporting point, an airway join, or entering special airspace;
  • 2–5 min before a level change, and again at the change.
All stations, FL340, airway UM603, position PETAK at 1512Z, estimating RONIX at 1538Z, FL340.
All stations, leaving FL340 now for FL360.

VATSIM doesn't ask for full enroute reports — on VATSIM you mainly announce around airports (below). A courtesy call at top of descent is welcome but not required.

Inbound / approach Both

Make a first inbound call around 25 NM out with your distance and planned runway, then build up to final.

KMIA traffic, PAC1235, 25 miles north, inbound ILS runway 09, KMIA.
KMIA traffic, PAC1235, 10 mile final, runway 09, KMIA.
KMIA traffic, PAC1235, turning final, runway 09, KMIA.
VFR traffic pattern (if flying VFR) Both

Announce each leg with the runway and direction.

KXYZ traffic, [callsign], joining left downwind runway 27, KXYZ.
KXYZ traffic, [callsign], base… then final, runway 27, KXYZ.
⚠️ You will usually get no reply to any of these. That's expected on Unicom — you're broadcasting so others can picture where you are, not asking permission. Keep flying normally.

One full PAC flight, KCVG → KMIA, no ATC the whole way:
1. KCVG — pushback C4, taxi rwy 36C
2. KCVG — lining up + departing 36C, southbound
3. KCVG — clear of the area, climbing (IVAO: add enroute reports)
4. KMIA — 25mi N, inbound ILS rwy 09
5. KMIA — final rwy 09
6. KMIA — clear rwy 09, taxi to cargo ramp

Summarised from each network's own guidance. Procedures can change — follow VATSIM's and IVAO's current rules and your pilot client's documentation. Sources: VATSIM blog “S.O.A.R – UNICOM”, IVAO wiki “UNICOM” and “Self-announcing procedure”.
