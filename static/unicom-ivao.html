Flying IVAO with no ATC IVAO

On IVAO, Unicom is a self-announcing system (their version of TIBA). When no controller is online you report your position and intentions — on the ground and enroute — so other pilots can avoid you.

UNICOM 122.800
⚡ Generate calls for my flight → On VATSIM instead? →

1 First: is anyone really off?

  • Check your pilot client for any controller serving your area or field. A higher position (APP, CTR) may still cover you — call them.
  • Only self-announce on 122.800 when no ATC is available.
  • Keep monitoring Unicom and re-check regularly — a controller can connect at any time, and other aircraft log on near you.
  • Read incoming text messages in the client; they may affect you.

2 IVAO conventions

  • Voice or text are both allowed on IVAO Unicom.
  • English only. All times in UTC (Zulu).
  • In text you don't type your callsign — the client adds it automatically.
  • No one acknowledges your calls unless there's a collision risk. Silence is normal.
  • Transponder on Charlie / TX and TCAS on, or others won't see you.
  • Keep messages simple; standard aviation abbreviations are fine.
Who to address: enroute, start with “All stations”. Near an aerodrome, use “[ICAO] traffic” (e.g. “OMDW traffic”). That's the one habit that tells IVAO pilots apart from VATSIM pilots.

The message, element by element

Build each enroute call from these. Drop any that aren't needed to stay clear.

When to announce

  • Any time traffic is within 20 NM (watch TCAS).
  • About 10 min before a reporting point, joining or crossing an ATS route/airway, or entering special airspace.
  • 2–5 min before a flight-level change, and again at the change.
  • Repeat your call periodically on a long leg — someone new may have logged on.

The calls, phase by phase

On the ground (push, taxi, line-up, departure)
OMDW traffic, pushing back stand A7, taxi for runway 30.
OMDW traffic, lining up and departing runway 30, following the filed SID.
OMDW traffic, airborne runway 30, climbing, leaving the circuit to the east.
Climb & enroute position reports

Switch to “All stations” once you leave the field's area.

All stations, climbing FL340, southbound, airway M677, position PEDUL at 1512Z, estimating NOSMI at 1538Z.
All stations, FL340, direct NOSMI to BOTAM, position NOSMI now, estimating BOTAM at 1604Z.
Flight-level changes

Call before (2–5 min out) and again at the change.

All stations, airway M677, leaving FL340 now for FL360.
All stations, maintaining FL360.
Descent, approach & landing

Back to “[ICAO] traffic” as you near the destination.

OOMS traffic, descending FL190, northwest, direct GOGLU to RONIX, estimating ILS runway 26L interception at 1620Z.
OOMS traffic, 25 miles north, inbound ILS runway 26L.
OOMS traffic, turning final, runway 26L.
OOMS traffic, clear of runway 26L, taxiing to the cargo ramp.
⚠️ Expect no replies. Self-announcing is one-way: you're painting a picture for anyone nearby, not asking permission. Keep flying the plan.
One full IVAO flight, OMDW → OOMS, no ATC:
  1. OMDW traffic — push A7, taxi rwy 30
  2. OMDW traffic — lining up + departing rwy 30, filed SID
  3. All stations — climbing FL340 S'bound, [airway], [fix] at [time]Z, est [next] [time]Z
  4. All stations — leaving FL340 for FL360 → maintaining FL360
  5. OOMS traffic — descending, est ILS 26L at [time]Z
  6. OOMS traffic — 25mi N, inbound ILS 26L → final 26L
  7. OOMS traffic — clear rwy 26L, taxi to ramp

Summarised from IVAO's own guidance; procedures can change — follow IVAO's current rules and your pilot client's documentation. Sources: IVAO wiki “UNICOM” and “Self-announcing procedure”. VATSIM differs (airport-focused, no enroute reports) — see the VATSIM sheet at /unicom.
