# Fonts

Bundled so PAC looks right offline and loads fast. Both are free under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(licence texts alongside), from the Google Fonts repository (github.com/google/fonts).

* **Barlow** by Jeremy Tribby. Body text in the Load Sheet design. `Barlow-OFL.txt`
* **Big Shoulders Stencil Display** by Patric King. Page titles, flight numbers and airport codes. `BigShouldersStencil-OFL.txt`
