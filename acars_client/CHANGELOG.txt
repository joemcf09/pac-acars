PAC ACARS - CHANGELOG
=====================

1.1.1 (November 2026)
---------------------
Fixed
- Quitting PAC ACARS during landing practice no longer asks about saving a
  flight: practice is never filed, so it just closes.

Good to know
- If you have 1.1.0, PAC ACARS offers this update when it opens: press
  Update now, then Install and restart.

1.1.0 (November 2026)
---------------------
New
- Updates from inside the app. When a new version is out, PAC ACARS asks when
  it opens: press Update now, then Install and restart. It downloads, installs
  and opens again by itself. Settings and saved flights are kept. It never
  asks while a flight is being tracked.
- Start-up screen with the PAC logo while PAC ACARS connects to the website.
- Moving map: your SimBrief route, the track you've flown and your aircraft,
  with heading, groundspeed, altitude and distance to go. Follow keeps the
  aircraft centered; Route shows the whole flight.
- Landing debrief right after touchdown: sink rate, runway, touchdown point,
  centerline, float and approach stability, with tips on what to do better.
- Landing practice: fly circuits or touch-and-goes at any airport. Every
  landing gets a debrief, and PAC ACARS counts how many in a row meet the
  checkride standard. Nothing is filed or sent to the website.
- Stable approach checks at 1,000 and 500 ft: a chime and a message if the
  gear is up, the sink rate is too high or the speed is wandering.
- SOP coach: friendly reminders from PAC's standard procedures (beacon before
  engine start, landing lights and strobes for takeoff, landing lights below
  10,000 ft). Reminders only, never scored. Turn it off in Settings.
- Fuel checks against your SimBrief plan at top of climb and top of descent.
- Top of descent reminder about 20 nm before you should start down.
- Occasional messages from PAC Operations on longer flights.
- Aircraft check: a message if the loaded aircraft doesn't look like the
  type you booked (wrong number of engines, or heavier than its max weight).
- Discord status: your profile shows the PAC flight you're flying. Turn it
  off in Settings.
- PAC ACARS now records bank angle and your lights, for the smooth-ride
  score and the SOP coach.

Changed
- The stencil lettering is gone: flight numbers and landing figures use the
  same solid lettering as the website.
- US spelling throughout (centerline, minimize, pressurization).

Good to know
- This is the last update you install by hand: download 1.1.0 from Downloads
  on the PAC website. From now on PAC ACARS updates itself.

1.0.8 (October 2026)
--------------------
Fixed
- Altitude now matches your cockpit altimeter. PAC ACARS used to show the
  aircraft's true height above sea level, which drifts away from the flight
  level when the air is warmer or colder than standard (for example 37,500 ft
  while cruising at FL360 on a warm day). It now shows what the altimeter
  shows, in the app, in the flight log and on the website's live map.
  Nothing else changes: landing, takeoff and approach checks still use the
  height above the ground, as before.

1.0.7 (October 2026)
--------------------
Fixed
- Arrival stands at airports that aren't PAC hubs. PAC ACARS used to make up a
  stand number there, which at the real airport was usually a passenger gate.
  It now sends you to the cargo ramp: park on any free cargo stand. PAC hubs
  (CVG, HKG, MIA, MXP, DWC, ANC) still give you PAC's own cargo stand.
- US spelling: "PAC CARGO CENTER STAND" at Cincinnati.

Good to know
- Update by downloading 1.0.7 from Downloads on the PAC website and installing
  over the old version. Saved flights and settings are kept.

1.0.6 (October 2026)
--------------------
Fixed
- Flights stuck on "Taxi in" with aircraft whose parking brake PAC ACARS
  can't read (seen with some X-Plane 11 add-ons). Stopped on the ground with
  all engines off for 60 seconds now puts you on block, brake or not. With a
  normal brake reading it still takes 5 seconds, as before.

New
- "File anyway" button once you've landed: if PAC ACARS doesn't go on block
  with your aircraft, you can still file. Those PIREPs go to staff review.
- Taxi-in hint: after landing, PAC ACARS tells you how to finish the flight
  (stop at the stand, parking brake set, engines off, keep PAC ACARS open).
- Closing the window during a flight now asks what you want: keep tracking
  in the system tray, or save the flight and quit (resume it later).

Good to know
- PAC ACARS is built and tested on MSFS 2024 and X-Plane 12. X-Plane 11 and
  MSFS 2020 should work; tell us in #pilot-help if something doesn't.
- Update by downloading 1.0.6 from Downloads on the PAC website and installing
  over the old version. Saved flights and settings are kept.
