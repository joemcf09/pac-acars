Standard Operating Procedures

PAC ACARS 1.2 · Revision November 2026

These are Pagani Air Cargo's standard procedures as PAC ACARS checks them. Reminders appear in the ACARS log as PAC SOP messages while you fly. Items marked Recorded are saved for your flight debrief and never change your grade. Items marked Reminder are prompts only. Items marked Graded affect your score.

1Before engine start and pushback

  • Beacon ON before engine start, and before pushback. Reminder
  • Load the aircraft type you booked. ACARS compares the engine count and warns if it does not match. Reminder
  • If ACARS warns that gross weight is above the maximum takeoff weight, check fuel and payload before you go. Reminder
  • MSFS: at pushback ACARS compares the simulator's center of gravity with the takeoff CG on your loadsheet. Reminder

2Engine start

ACARS logs every confirmed start (ENG 1 START) and, once all engines are running, the order and the time from the first start to the last. Engines that were already running when tracking began are not counted.

3Taxi, single-engine taxi and block time

  • Keep taxi speed at or below 30 knots. Each time ACARS sees more, it costs 3 points. Graded
  • If you taxi out on one engine of a multi-engine aircraft, ACARS records it. This needs the simulator to report the state of every engine. Recorded

Your debrief checks time in separate steps, so you can see where minutes went. Recorded

  • Departure: leaving the gate within {{GRACE_DEP}} minutes either side of the planned out time from your SimBrief plan.
  • Taxi out and taxi in: inside the allowance for the airport size.
  • Airborne: within 5 minutes, or 10 percent, of the planned enroute time.
  • Arrival: measured from takeoff. Takeoff plus the planned enroute time plus the taxi-in allowance, with {{GRACE_ARR}} minutes of margin, so a late departure does not hide how the flight itself went.
  • Block: total block against the plan, with a note on how much came from airborne time and how much from taxiing.

5Climb, descent and approach

  • 250 knots indicated or less below FL100. Exceeding it costs 5 points. Graded
  • Stable approach gates at 1,000 ft and 500 ft above the ground: gear down, flaps set by 1,000 ft, sink rate not above 1,000 fpm, and speed within 15 knots over the last 12 seconds. If a gate fails, ACARS calls UNSTABLE AT 1000FT or 500FT and suggests a go-around. Reminder
  • Fuel is checked against your SimBrief plan at top of climb and top of descent. Reminder

6What changes your grade

Graded

  • Landing rate and landing load
  • Trip fuel well over plan, and landing below reserve fuel
  • Overweight landing and tail strike
  • Bounced landings, taxi speed, 250 knot limit
  • Slew mode or position jumps, and takeoff without a gate departure
  • Late delivery: more than 35 percent over the planned block time, or 25 percent on Critical and High priority flights. Delays you report to dispatch are excused.

Recorded only

  • Engine start order and start time
  • Single-engine taxi
  • Light positions at the gate and on the stand
  • Taxi allowances and the departure, airborne, arrival and block checks
  • PAC SOP reminders
Pagani Air Cargo is a virtual airline for flight simulation. These procedures are for simulation only and are not for real-world aviation use. Reference values are reviewed as flights are flown and may change.
